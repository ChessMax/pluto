# A text step

A `text` step is content and nothing else — no answer to check, no extra front
matter keys. Everything below the front matter is Markdown, rendered to the
subset of HTML that Stepik accepts.

This step carries `label: intro`, which makes it a stable target for in-course
links. Other steps point at it by that name rather than by its position, so
renumbering the course does not break them.

The heading above is the step's first line, so it renders centred — one of the
two transformations the renderer applies that you did not write.
